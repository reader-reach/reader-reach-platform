About Reader Reach

Books are better when they find the right people.

Reader Reach is designed around a simple idea: a great book does not need to appeal to everyone. It needs to reach the readers who will value it.

We create a space where authors can clearly present their books and readers can discover stories that match their interests.

“The goal isn't simply to help people find more books. It's to help them find the right books.”
